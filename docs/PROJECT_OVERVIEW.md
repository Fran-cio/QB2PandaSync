# Project Overview

## Project Name
**QB2PandaSync** (working name: *Invoice Bridge*)

## Goal
Automate the transfer of invoice data from QuickBooks to PandaDoc Payments, reducing manual operations and payment delays.

## Business Context
The current team workflow requires manual PDF export and manual upload. This creates avoidable friction and operational risk.

## Desired Outcome
- Faster invoice sending
- Fewer manual errors
- Better visibility into invoice/payment status
- Clear audit trail from invoice creation to payment completion

## Scope (Initial)
- Fetch invoice data from QuickBooks
- Create a payment document in PandaDoc
- Assign payer and amount
- Send invoice automatically
- Track resulting status

## Out of Scope (Initial)
- Full accounting reconciliation automation
- Multi-ERP support
- Advanced analytics

## Stakeholders
- Finance operations
- Sales operations
- Customer success (when payer follow-up is needed)

## Success Metrics
- Manual invoice processing time reduced by at least 70%
- Duplicate invoice incidents reduced to near zero
- 95%+ successful sync rate
