# Current Process (Manual)

## Summary
Invoices are currently moved from QuickBooks to PandaDoc manually as PDF files.

## Steps (As-Is)
1. Open QuickBooks: `Sales & get paid > Invoices`
2. Select the invoice
3. Print and save as PDF
4. Open PandaDoc: `Payments > Add document`
5. Upload the PDF
6. Click `$` to configure payment details
7. Assign payer and amount
8. Click `Send invoice`
9. Wait for email confirmation from PandaDoc

## Pain Points
- Repetitive manual work
- Risk of selecting wrong invoice file
- Risk of wrong payer or amount
- Limited cross-system traceability
- Not scalable as invoice volume grows

## Operational Risks
- Delayed customer billing
- Human error in key financial fields
- Missing visibility when issues occur
