# How-To Guide (Non-Technical)

## Who Is This For?
This guide is for finance and operations users who need to understand what the integration does and how to work with it.

## What Will Change for You?
Before: you exported invoices as PDF and uploaded them manually.
After: invoices are sent to PandaDoc automatically once created in QuickBooks.

## Day-to-Day Steps

### 1) Create the invoice in QuickBooks
Use your normal billing process.

### 2) Confirm invoice data is complete
Please verify:
- Customer (payer) name
- Invoice amount
- Due date

### 3) Wait for automatic delivery
The system sends invoice data to PandaDoc automatically.

### 4) Check status
A status will show whether the invoice was:
- Sent
- Opened
- Paid
- Failed (needs attention)

## If Something Fails
If an invoice is marked as **Failed**:
1. Open the error details in the operations panel (or report)
2. Correct the missing/incorrect data in QuickBooks
3. Trigger resend (or wait for scheduled retry)

## Common Questions

### Do I still need to upload PDF files?
No, not in the automated flow.

### Can I still process one manually if needed?
Yes. Manual fallback remains available for urgent exceptions.

### Who should I contact if invoices are not sending?
Contact the internal project owner or operations support team.

## Good Practices
- Always use complete payer data
- Avoid editing key fields repeatedly after sending
- Review failed items daily until the process stabilizes
